Figure 1. The ten environments.
Each panel is a rectilinear (pinhole) view cut from the 360-degree equirectangular panorama shown to participants, so that the scene looks as it would through a normal lens rather than in panoramic distortion. Panels are ordered by human negative affect, lowest first (top-left: scene 8, Cafe, NA 1.30; bottom-right: scene 9, Service car park, NA 3.06). Above each panel: scene number and short name. Below each panel: the human mean PA (blue, left) and NA (orange, right) over the 84 participants and 20 items. PA and NA are on the 1-5 scale.
Faces and licence plates are blurred (automatic Haar face detection plus manual boxes, analysis/scene_info.csv). The thumbnails are re-encoded and carry no EXIF, XMP or GPS metadata. The view direction and field of view were chosen to show the dominant content of each scene and are not what the models saw: models received the full panorama.

Units and conventions. Units throughout: 200 image x item cells (10 environments x 20 PANAS items); N = 84 participants; 10 independent runs per model and image; 43 models (two aliased Gemini models excluded). ICC = ICC(2,1), two-way random, absolute agreement, between the human cell means and the model cell means. Tiers: low < .70, mid .70-.80, top >= .80.
