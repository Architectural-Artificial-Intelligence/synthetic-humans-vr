Figure S2. Representational similarity of the environments.
Each matrix is the 10 x 10 representational dissimilarity matrix (RDM) of the environments: entry (i, j) is the correlation distance (1 - Pearson r) between the 20-item rating profiles of environments i and j. Left: human means (84 participants); right: the mean of 10 runs of Gemini 4 Argon. Rows and columns are in the order of Figure 1 (by human negative affect, lowest first), so low-NA environments are at the top-left. Both panels share one colour scale (darker = more dissimilar).
The block structure is the same in both: the six low-NA environments (8, 7, 6, 4, 1, 2) are similar to each other and clearly different from the four high-NA environments (5, 10, 3, 9). The Spearman correlation between the two RDMs (45 pairs) is the representational-similarity score; other distance measures (Euclidean, cosine) are in the RSA tables.

Units and conventions. Units throughout: 200 image x item cells (10 environments x 20 PANAS items); N = 84 participants; 10 independent runs per model and image; 43 models (two aliased Gemini models excluded). ICC = ICC(2,1), two-way random, absolute agreement, between the human cell means and the model cell means. Tiers: low < .70, mid .70-.80, top >= .80.
