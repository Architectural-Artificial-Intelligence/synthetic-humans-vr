Figure 5. Model ranking with two kinds of uncertainty.
All 43 models, sorted by ICC(2,1) (open marker). Label colour and marker shape = provider. Thick bar: 95% percentile bootstrap CI when participants and runs are resampled (B = 2000, participants resampled as clusters, scenes fixed). Thin bar: 95% CI when the ten environments are resampled instead (participants and runs fixed). The second interval answers a different question: how well would the ranking generalise to other environments? It is a median 3.0 times wider than the first, because the ten environments, not the 84 participants or 10 runs, are the dominant limit on what can be concluded.
Dashed line: the scene-blind item-mean baseline (ICC 0.57). Dotted lines: tier cut-offs .70 and .80. Star: the paired margin over the baseline (model ICC minus baseline ICC, with environments, participants and runs resampled jointly) has a 95% CI that excludes zero; this is the case for 9 of 43 models.
Overlapping thin bars mean most neighbours in the ranking cannot be separated: the best model's rank interval is 1-2 and its environment interval overlaps all other models'. The ordering within a tier should not be interpreted.

Units and conventions. Units throughout: 200 image x item cells (10 environments x 20 PANAS items); N = 84 participants; 10 independent runs per model and image; 43 models (two aliased Gemini models excluded). ICC = ICC(2,1), two-way random, absolute agreement, between the human cell means and the model cell means. Tiers: low < .70, mid .70-.80, top >= .80.
