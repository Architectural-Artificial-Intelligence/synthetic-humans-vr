Figure S1. Run-to-run stability.
Left: ICC(2,1) with human cell means as a function of the number of runs averaged (k = 1..10), for every model (grey lines), with Gemini 4 Argon in blue. For each k the ICC is the mean over random subsets of k runs. Averaging helps only slightly: the median model goes from 0.785 with one run to 0.792 with ten, and Argon from 0.858 to 0.865. Curves are flat from about k = 3, so 10 runs are far more than needed for the group-level estimates reported here.
Right: variance decomposition of the model ratings (mean over the 43 models) in a crossed image x item x run design: items 51%, image x item 43%, image 1.9%, run-to-run and residual 3.5% (medians 50.5%, 42.8%, 1.6%, 2.4%). Almost all variation comes from which item is rated and from how each item varies across images; sampling noise from the temperature is a small component. Median inter-run ICC is 0.975; split-half reliability of the model mean is 0.985.

Units and conventions. Units throughout: 200 image x item cells (10 environments x 20 PANAS items); N = 84 participants; 10 independent runs per model and image; 43 models (two aliased Gemini models excluded). ICC = ICC(2,1), two-way random, absolute agreement, between the human cell means and the model cell means. Tiers: low < .70, mid .70-.80, top >= .80.
